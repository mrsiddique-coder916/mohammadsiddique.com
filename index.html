Why people buy investment property

The usual reasons, each with a number you should check before you buy.

  • Rent comes in every month

    A rented property produces income while you hold it. The numbers that matter are the rent it can realistically earn and what it costs to own.

  • Someone else helps pay it down

    Rent can cover part or all of the loan payment, so the balance drops over time while you keep the property.

  • A loan stretches your down payment

    You can control a whole property with a fraction of its price. That raises both the possible gain and the risk, so the loan has to fit the income.

  • Property can gain value over a long hold

    Prices here have moved up and down from year to year, so plan to hold long enough to ride out a dip.

  • The tax code treats rentals differently

    Depreciation, deductible expenses, and 1031 exchanges can change what you keep. Your tax advisor decides how they apply to you.

  • You can add value yourself

    A remodel, an added unit, or better management can raise what a property earns. This is the part I have done myself for 35 years.

Real estate can lose value, sit vacant, and need costly repairs. Nothing here is tax, legal, or financial advice, and no return is guaranteed. Talk to your tax and financial advisors before you buy.

Questions people ask first

Where do you work?

Across the Sacramento Valley, including Sacramento and Elk Grove, and in the Bay Area. If you are not sure whether your property is in range, call and ask.

What kinds of property do you handle?

Single-family homes, duplexes to fourplexes, apartment buildings with five or more units, mixed-use and commercial buildings, restaurant and retail leases, and vacant land for development.

Can you help me buy my first investment property?

Yes. I have owned and remodeled rental property for more than 35 years, so we can look at a property the way an owner would: what it rents for, what it needs, and what it costs to hold.

Do you work with 1031 exchanges?

I help investors plan a sale and the replacement purchase around an exchange, and I have used exchanges on my own properties. The exchange itself runs through a qualified intermediary, and your tax advisor should confirm how the rules apply to you.
