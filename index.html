Questions agents ask first

Why a team instead of a big national brokerage?

Local training and support from an agent who still works deals, a platform that handles the file admin with you approving everything, and no desk, platform or software fee. You are one of a few people here, not one of tens of thousands.

Who is the broker?

FX Realty, a licensed California real estate corporation, CalDRE #02161418. The broker of record is George Tung, CalDRE #01251439, who reviews and approves every file, every new agent and every commission payout.

What does the platform actually do?

Transaction files with checklists and deadlines, the broker's approval queue, lead routing and follow-up tracking, client portals, and AI-drafted messages and documents that you review and send under your own name. It does not make licensed decisions; you do.

What is the split?

Set in writing with the broker before you join. There is no cap, no desk fee and no platform fee. We go through the numbers on the first call.

Do I bring my clients and listings?

Your sphere is yours. Listings and client agreements move only with your current broker's release and each client's written consent, and the same is true if you ever leave.

Is there a non-compete?

No. The agreement relies on confidentiality only. You can work with any broker after you leave.
