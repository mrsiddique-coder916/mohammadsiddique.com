About Mohammad Siddique

Real Estate Broker · Principal Investor · Commercial Development Advisor · Sacramento, California

23+ Years Licensed

Institutional discipline. Ownership-level perspective.

Mohammad Siddique brings nearly two decades of collaborative experience in real estate acquisition, investment, and development, with a track record spanning residential investment property, commercial transactions, 1031 tax-deferred exchanges, and long-hold cash-flowing portfolios.

His work spans the full transaction lifecycle — from property listing and acquisition to disposition and long-term asset management — with a specialized focus on commercial development over the past 15 years, including commercial, residential, mixed-use, hotel/hospitality, and student housing projects.

CalDRE License #01372236 — issued March 2003. Held under Angel Realty & Developments Inc., doing business as Asset Bridge Realty, CalDRE #02295075.

35+ Years, Hands-On

A remodeler and rental property owner first.

Before the brokerage license, before commercial development — Mohammad was buying, remodeling, and renting property himself. That hands-on experience still shapes every transaction he's part of today.

"With more than 35 years of experience as a rental property owner and remodeling investor, I've personally executed the buy-remodel-rent-1031 exchange cycle many times over — it gives me firsthand insight into the concerns of the investors and property owners I represent."
